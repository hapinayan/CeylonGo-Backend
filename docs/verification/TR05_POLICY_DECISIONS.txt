T-03 | Vehicle catalogue | Product and database owners
Question: What are the two additional approved category identifiers, labels
and aliases? Which legacy rows, rate plans, eligibility rules, search filters
and client enum values map to each category?
Existing: bike, bicycle and tuk_tuk in public/provider schemas and SQL.
Proposals: Introduce distinct types while preserving existing IDs; or approve
explicit subtype mappings under existing types if they satisfy the catalogue.
Recommendation: Keep the three current identifiers until a versioned catalogue
and explicit legacy mapping are approved. Prefer an additive mapping.
Impact: app/schemas/rental_vehicle.py, app/schemas/rental_provider.py,
app/routers/rental_vehicles.py, app/services/rental_eligibility.py,
migrations/019_create_rental_tables.sql and migrations/046_create_rental_age_rules.sql.
Tests: test_get_categories_returns_supported_types_and_fleet_counts,
test_category_specs_validation_* and new old/new client round-trip cases.

A-04 | Provider business verification | Product and Operations
Question: Which evidence types are required for each rental business type?
Who can review them, with what reason, expiry and resubmission states? Does
individual evidence approval gate provider approval, a badge, or publication?
Existing: Shared provider approval checks identity document presence; admin
can inspect protected identity documents. Existing business fields do not
define an independent evidence decision.
Proposals: Reuse onboarding review with documented business evidence gates;
or use an independently auditable review linked to onboarding and badges.
Recommendation: Reuse protected evidence access and retain a separate audit
decision for each approved business-evidence type.
Impact: app/routers/admin_providers.py, app/models/provider_profile.py and
app/routers/rental_providers.py. Tests: test_secure_identity_document_viewing,
test_approve_provider_success and a new business evidence decision matrix.

T-14 | Cancellation terms | Product and Finance
Question: What structured rule maps the published policy to cutoff, fee,
rounding and treatment of unpaid, cash and captured-online reservations?
Which historical snapshots may use a default and who handles disputes?
Existing: Booking snapshots retain policy text, hours and daily rate. Zero
hours and exact cutoff are handled. Existing numeric late-fee logic remains;
refund reservations are limited to the verified capture and held amounts.
Cancellation returns 409 for a reservation recorded in a different currency
or with a negative amount, before any cancellation or refund change persists.
Proposals: Publish versioned structured templates; or require explicit
structured terms alongside prose and hold ambiguous legacy cases for review.
Recommendation: Version structured terms for new bookings and route ambiguous
legacy terms to authorized review. Approve the formula before changing fees.
Impact: app/routers/rental_bookings.py, app/schemas/rental_booking.py and
app/models/rental_booking.py. Tests: test_cancellation_keeps_booking_time_cutoff,
test_rental_cancellation_reserves_only_remaining_verified_capture and
test_rental_cancel_and_capture_serialized_outcomes.

X-01 and X-11 | Licence and permit evidence | Compliance and Operations
Question: What licence classes, issuers, jurisdictions and endorsements are
accepted by category at booking and handover? Are permits alternatives or
cumulative? What are their validity, review and protected-access rules?
Existing: Motorized rentals require approved evidence covering the return
date. The current foreign tuk-tuk check requires an approved eligibility
record with an IDP number or recognition permit number on file. The source
X-01 access wording is truncated.
Proposals: Adopt a documented category/jurisdiction verification table;
or require authorized manual review for unsupported combinations.
Recommendation: Obtain the compliance-approved table and require authorized
review of combinations it does not cover before extending the automated rule.
Impact: app/routers/rental_eligibility.py, app/services/rental_eligibility.py,
app/models/rental.py, booking and handover routes. Tests:
test_secure_document_access_and_path_traversal_protection,
test_ar11_foreign_tuk_tuk_aac_recognition_permit_verification and
test_handover_rechecks_license_eligibility_and_sanitizes_response.

X-06 | Inspection photos and ownership | Product, Operations and Storage
Question: Which evidence is mandatory at each stage and under which exceptions?
Which upload, owner and booking IDs prove a photo belongs to an inspection?
Which capture/check-in clock and retention period are authoritative?
Existing: Reports support optional photo references, timestamps, odometer
and authenticated traveller acknowledgement. Reference validation does not
establish ownership in an external upload system.
Proposals: Bind authorized uploads to booking and inspection IDs; or permit
approved external storage with explicit ownership proof and review.
Recommendation: Bind managed upload IDs to owner, booking and inspection.
Approve any external-reference exception and its retention rules separately.
Impact: app/routers/rental_bookings.py, app/routers/rental_providers.py and
app/schemas/rental_provider.py. Tests: test_record_vehicle_handover_success_and_lifecycle,
test_return_inspection_cannot_be_acknowledged_via_handover_endpoint and
new upload binding/retention cases when the contract is approved.

X-10 | Seating and solo self-drive | Compliance and Product
Question: What authoritative total/usable seats apply per jurisdiction and
vehicle, including the driver? Is zero passengers valid for solo self-drive?
What should happen to existing listings or bookings outside the approved rule?
Existing: Supported tuk-tuk listings have three or four seats including the
driver, with a minimum of one passenger. Public and booking validation agree,
and typed party size coexists with historical fallback.
Proposals: Validate per-vehicle verified capacity; or apply an approved
category rule with documented exceptions and legacy handling.
Recommendation: Prefer verified vehicle-specific capacity where evidence is
available. Keep existing values until Compliance approves limits and solo use.
Impact: app/core/rental_capacity.py, app/routers/rental_vehicles.py,
app/routers/rental_bookings.py and migrations/044_add_rental_booking_party_size.sql.
Tests: test_tuktuk_capacity_is_consistent_in_discovery_detail_quote_and_booking,
test_get_rental_booking_retrieves_persisted_agreement_and_passengers and
new approved solo and regulatory boundaries.
