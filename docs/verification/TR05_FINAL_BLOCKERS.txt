TR-05 external release gates

Approved policy required

T-03: Product must supply two approved category identifiers, labels, aliases
and legacy mappings.
T-14: Product and Finance must approve a published cancellation fee mapping,
rounding and cash/online treatment, including ambiguous historical snapshots.
A-04: Operations must approve business evidence types, decision states,
review permissions, expiry and provider approval/badge effects.
X-01: Compliance must supply approved licence classes, required evidence
and access rules. The source access criterion is truncated.
X-06: Product and Storage must approve mandatory pickup/return evidence,
upload ownership proof, exceptions and retention.
X-10: Compliance and Product must approve jurisdiction-specific seats,
the driver-seat convention, solo self-drive and legacy handling.
X-11: Compliance must approve applicable foreign permit/endorsement
combinations and how to validate them at booking and handover.
T-18: Product must confirm whether scheduled reminders are required and
their timing and recipients.
X-07: Product and Finance must confirm whether booked grace and hourly rates
remain immutable when a provider edits a rate plan.

Authorized isolated SQL Server required

The DBA must first authorize read-only access for the approved Windows
principal and isolated host/database, with ODBC Driver 18 available on
that host. Verify identity, the ledger through 042 and the catalog using
TR05_SQLSERVER_PREFLIGHT_REPORT.txt. Migration writes through 046, a
recoverable checkpoint, the 11 staging cases and independent-connection
fixtures require separate permission under TR05_STAGING_RUNBOOK.txt.

T-10 / P-08 / P-11: Prove booking overlap, both blackout writer paths,
and provider acceptance against expiry/capture with separate connections.
T-11 / T-12 / P-15: Verify capture/cancellation atomicity, cash collection
and same-key retries against the real transaction ledger.
T-18: Verify event and notification rollback together on a SQL connection
and verify recipient isolation in deployed roles.
P-13 / P-14 / X-09: Verify licensed self-drive handover and return, evidence
and signed acceptance after SQL persistence.
P-16 / A-06 / X-04 / X-13: Prove online and cash receipts, deposits, refund
reserves and payouts reconcile to authorized financial evidence. Verify
competing refund decisions yield one decision, audit entry and traveller alert
using independent SQL Server connections.
A-01: Rehearse rental provider approval/rejection and protected document
access with migrated SQL Server and deployed role assignments.
X-02 / X-03 / X-12: Verify migrated age rules, helmet metadata and private
insurance evidence with historical and current records.

Authorized PayHere sandbox required

T-11 / T-14 / T-19 / P-16 / P-17 / A-06 / A-07 / X-13:
Authorize a sandbox merchant, approved callback host, disposable synthetic
accounts and the isolated persistence needed for PH-01 through PH-05.
Supply settings via the approved secret channel. Follow
TR05_PAYHERE_SANDBOX_CHECKLIST.txt and record signed callback and ledger
evidence. PH-05 additionally needs an authorized refund execution mechanism
or verified manual settlement evidence.

Angular and Flutter environments required

T-06 / T-18 / T-19 / P-13 / P-14 / P-15 / A-07 / X-02 / X-03 / X-09 / X-12:
Provide the client projects or running acceptance builds and test accounts.
Replay TR05_CLIENT_CONTRACT_FIXTURES.json plus the staging runbook checklist.
Verify maps, published status and labels, alert navigation, signed inspection
state, client key reuse and the display of age, helmet and insurance data.
Repeat category and seating acceptance once T-03 and X-10 are approved.
